HUONG DAN CAC PHAN BO SUNG (TIEU CHI 26-35)

1. TEN DANH MUC TREN QUAN LY SAN PHAM
- API GET api/products/paged tra them CategoryName.
- WinForms hien cot "Ten danh muc"; CategoryId duoc an tren bang, nhung van duoc giu trong model de cap nhat san pham dung khoa ngoai.
- ComboBox them/sua san pham hien CategoryName va gui CategoryId ve API.

2. ASNOTRACKING
- Bo sung AsNoTracking cho cac truy van GET chi doc trong Products, Categories, Customers, Users va Orders o cac vi tri da chinh sua.

3. JWT VA PHAN QUYEN
- Products, Categories, Customers, Orders va Users yeu cau dang nhap JWT.
- Quan ly/sua/xoa san pham, danh muc, khach hang va tai khoan: Admin.
- Checkout: Admin hoac Cashier.
- Bao cao doanh thu theo ca: Admin.
- Swagger da co Bearer Authorize. Dang nhap qua POST api/auth/login, sao chep token va bam Authorize, nhap token (khong can tu them chu Bearer neu giao dien da tu them).

4. STORED PROCEDURE DOANH THU THEO CA
- Mo SQL Server Management Studio, chon dung database TranThiThaoNhuDb.
- Mo va chay file Scripts/ShiftRevenueReport.sql mot lan.
- API moi: GET /api/orders/shift-revenue?from=2026-10-24T08:00:00&to=2026-10-24T12:00:00
- Chi Admin duoc goi endpoint nay. from phai nho hon to. Endpoint tinh so hoa don va tong doanh thu trong khoang [from, to).

5. CAC PHAN DA CO SAN VA GIU NGUYEN
- JWT login, Swagger Bearer, ADO.NET tim kiem san pham bang Stored Procedure SearchProductsByKeyword, SqlDataReader/SqlParameter, va transaction thanh toan duoc giu lai.

LUU Y
- Cac thay doi trong ZIP chua duoc build/test trong moi truong nay vi khong co .NET SDK. Sau khi mo bang Visual Studio, Build Solution truoc khi chay.
- Vi phan quyen duoc ap dung nghiem hon, dang nhap bang tai khoan Admin de quan ly san pham/danh muc/khach hang/tai khoan; dang nhap Cashier de ban hang va doc danh sach san pham/danh muc.
